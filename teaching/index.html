Teaching

Revision guides and practice material I wrote while tutoring STAT1011 — Statistics for Social Sciences at VinUniversity, Spring 2026. The curriculum follows OpenStax Introductory Statistics.

  • A Summary of Statistics for Social Sciences

    Condensed notes across the whole course, written to be read before an exam.

  • Week 1 – Week 4 Revision

    Multiple-choice and written questions with worked solutions, from sampling and data onwards.

  • Midterm Revision Test 1

    A mock midterm paper with worked answers.

  • Hypothesis Testing: One Sample and Two Samples

    Concept summaries, decision flowcharts, rejection regions and p-values, and step-by-step procedures.

  • The Chi-Square Distribution

    Goodness-of-fit, independence, homogeneity and single-variance tests, with a formula sheet and answer keys.

  • Linear Regression and Correlation

    Chapter 12: scatter plots, the correlation coefficient, and fitting and reading a regression line.

  • F Distribution and One-Way ANOVA

    Chapter 13 revision guide.

  • Final Exam Revision Guide

    Comprehensive coverage of Chapters 1 through 13, with concept summaries and strategies for every chapter.
